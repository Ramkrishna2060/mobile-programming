### Krishi-Bazaar Direct (Micro-Agri Intermediary Bypass & Price Tracker)
### Introduction
Krishi-Bazaar Direct is a mobile application designed to bridge the gap between rural agricultural producers and urban buyers in Nepal. Agriculture remains the backbone of the Nepalese economy, yet the sector struggles with supply chain inefficiencies, lack of price transparency, and heavy reliance on intermediaries. By leveraging React Native for cross-platform mobile access, Firebase for real-time data synchronization and offline capabilities, and an embedded HTML/CSS/jQuery web engine for dynamic visual price charting, Krishi-Bazaar Direct provides a direct, transparent digital marketplace and price-tracking ecosystem tailored to Nepal's local agricultural landscape.
### Problem Statement
The agricultural supply chain in Nepal suffers from systemic inefficiencies, starting with multi-layered networks of middlemen (bicholiya) who exploit smallholder farmers by offering them a minimal fraction of the final retail price. This issue is compounded by severe price asymmetry, as farmers lack access to daily wholesale market rates from hubs like Kalimati and are forced to accept unfair rates set by local traders. On the receiving end, urban businesses and consumer groups in cities like Kathmandu, Pokhara, and Chitwan face inflated fresh produce prices due to a lack of direct procurement channels to farming cooperatives. Furthermore, the absence of advance demand forecasting and coordinated logistics leads to heavy post-harvest losses, causing substantial volumes of produce to rot before reaching urban markets.
### Objectives
The primary goal of Krishi-Bazaar Direct is to build a real-time mobile platform connecting rural farming cooperatives directly with urban bulk buyers to reduce intermediary dependence and stabilize pricing across Nepal. To achieve this, the platform integrates a live market rate index powered by Firebase to display daily wholesale produce prices across various districts. It facilitates direct trade by allowing farmers to list upcoming harvests while urban buyers initiate collective group orders, backed by embedded HTML, CSS, and jQuery micro-dashboards that render interactive price-trend analytics. Finally, the app ensures accessibility in remote areas through Firebase's low-bandwidth offline caching and leverages React Native geolocation services to pair buyers with the nearest farming clusters, effectively minimizing transportation costs and travel time.
